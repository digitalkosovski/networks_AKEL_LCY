# Networks AKEL - LCY

## Description
Network data about contacts between the Yugoslav and Cypriot communist parties in the 1960s and 1970s.

## Archives and data
The data was produced on the basis of Yugoslav archival sources: 
* Archive of Yugoslavia, Fond of the League of Communists of Yugoslavia, International Commission, Cyprus.
* Archive of Yugoslavia, Fond of Tanjug, photographic collection.

Data was collected on encounters between Cypriot and Yugoslav communists from the early 1960s to the mid 1970s, the period covered by available sources, that were registered by the League of Communists of Yugoslavia in reports and minutes. An examination of the photographic collection of Tanjug, Yugoslavia's press agency, rendered complementary data on encounters that had not left archival traces.

The data was initially storaged on a [Heurist](https://heuristnetwork.org/5417/10746) relational database containing detailed data about meetings and participants from both political parties. It was exported as a .gefx graph file of a *two-mode network* of **meetings** and **individuals**. The network was imported on Gephi and projected to a *one-mode network* of **individuals** (linked by common participation in meetings). Nodes and edges files were created from this projection.

## Contents of the repository

The repository contains:
* The nodes file of the one-mode projection.
* The edges file of the one-mode projection.
* Sample visualizations from Gephi: the graph on a Yifan Hu layout; three ego-networks (Veljko Vlahovic, Bosko Siljegovic, and Ezekeias Pappaioanou).
